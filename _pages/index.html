California State University, Long Beach · Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering

Multiphase and Complex Fluids Flow Lab

We build accurate and tractable computational models for polydisperse multiphase flows, turbulent mixing and reacting flows, from nanoparticle reactors and ultrasonic separators to rocket nozzles, truck brake-wear plumes and lunar landing plumes.
