Biography

Ehsan Madadi is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at California State University, Long Beach, and the founder and director of the Multiphase and Complex Fluids Flow (MCF2) Lab, established in 2020. The lab develops, implements and validates computational models for polydisperse multiphase, turbulent mixing and reacting flows.

Prof. Madadi received a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering, with a minor in Applied Scientific Computing (high-performance computing), from Iowa State University in 2017, advised by Alberto Passalacqua and Rodney O. Fox. The dissertation developed quadrature-based models for multiphase and turbulent mixing and reacting flows, including the extended quadrature method of moments with log-normal kernels and moment solutions of the Fokker–Planck mixing model.

As a postdoctoral fellow at The University of Texas at Austin, Prof. Madadi worked with Ben R. Hodges at the Center for Water and the Environment on a conservative finite-volume engine for EPA's Storm Water Management Model, later released as SWMM5+, and with Mukul Sharma in Petroleum and Geosystems Engineering on choke management to stabilize well production.

At CSULB, the lab's research is supported by the National Science Foundation, the ACS Petroleum Research Fund, NASA, the Army Research Office and the California State University. Prof. Madadi collaborates with Prof. Roberto Putzu at HEPIA, the University of Applied Sciences and Arts Western Switzerland in Geneva; HEPIA bachelor's students complete their thesis research in the MCF2 Lab under joint supervision. Prof. Madadi has supervised graduate, undergraduate honors and international visiting students, and serves as a reviewer for scientific journals and funding agencies.
