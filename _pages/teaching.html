Scientific computing workshops

With support from the California Space Grant Consortium and the CSULB BUILD program, the lab runs hands-on workshops in Python, Git, Linux and high-performance computing that are open to undergraduate and graduate students across campus, led by Prof. Madadi with student instructors.

  • Scientific Computing Workshop for STEM Education, California Space Grant Consortium (2023)
  • Scientific Computing Basics and Needs for Health-Related Research Careers, CSULB BUILD (2023 – 2024)
  • Expanding Diversity in Aerospace through High-Performance Computing: a dual-session workshop series, California Space Grant Consortium (2024 – 2025)

Research students meet weekly with Prof. Madadi and work in a shared team channel. Current students and alumni are listed on the People page.
